A learner who teaches

My approach starts with belonging.

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Education with Early Childhood–6 ESL Certification at The University of Texas at Arlington. My work centers on dynamic, standards-aligned lesson plans that invite teamwork, problem-solving, and inclusive growth for learners ages 3–12.

Across tutoring at Tutor of Dragons in Southlake, leading active youth classes at the Hurst Recreation Center, caregiving, and community recreation, I practice classroom management, active listening, and conflict resolution every single day.

I am growing toward a classroom where culture is valued, communication is clear, and every learner has a real path into the lesson.

↳ Outside of teaching, I play tennis, string rackets with precision, work out, and read educational literature.
